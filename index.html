How it works

From goods-in to good deed,
in four steps.

MediTrack turns expiry anxiety into a closed-loop workflow your whole team — and your patients — can see.

STEP 01

Stock in with batch & expiry

Every purchase entry captures batch no., quantity, MRP, manufacturer and expiry — validated and duplication-checked automatically.

STEP 02

QR minted per batch

A unique QR + human verify-code is generated instantly. Print, stick, scan — full traceability from warehouse to counter.

STEP 03

Daily expiry sweep

Each morning the engine re-scores every batch, pushes 90/30/7-day and expired alerts, and quantifies rupee-risk for owners.

STEP 04

Donate before it expires

Surplus is listed, NGOs request it, pharmacies approve & dispatch — with a live timeline until delivery is confirmed.
